# SheRISE — Eight Design Principles

Apply to every screen. When you disagree during code review, the principles win.

## 01 · Dignity before data

A participant is a person on a journey — not a case number. Copy addresses her by name, celebrates her wins, and never labels her by her past. Row IDs, cohort codes, and category tags stay in tooltips and admin views. Public-facing surfaces read like a magazine profile, not a dashboard record.

## 02 · Consent is a first-class feature

Cross-posting to Facebook or LinkedIn is **off by default**. What SheRISE says publicly is what the user authored. Program history is **never disclosed** in system-generated copy. If a copy string ever mentions "correctional," "reintegration," or "program," it must be rewritten before shipping.

## 03 · Offline-first, low-data

Every lesson downloads. Every post drafts locally. The app must work when a woman is on 2G in a community centre and finishes at home. Assume connectivity is a luxury, not a baseline. Video defaults to 480p; user can opt into HD only on wifi.

## 04 · Legible on small screens

- Body text ≥ 14px, hero copy ≥ 22px.
- Hit targets ≥ 44px.
- Contrast never below WCAG AA.
- Icons always paired with labels (except bottom tab where labels sit under icons).
- This app is not for design showcases — decoration must earn its place.

## 05 · Trainers are visible

A verified trainer badge is the difference between advice and noise. Trainer badges appear on avatars (checkmark) and inline (gold "Trainer · Verified" pill). Trainer notes on participant progress are **private to the participant** — never broadcast to circles, never syndicated to social.

## 06 · Funder-legible dashboards

The admin surface reports what a funder needs to sign a cheque: reach, retention, first-income, and community perception — with export-ready formats. Numbers are large and unambiguous. Drop-off funnels show absolute counts alongside percentages. Every download is audited.

## 07 · Named reactions, not likes

"Cheer, hold, celebrate" instead of anonymous hearts. Language shapes culture; a supportive community needs supportive verbs. Never render `24 likes` — always `24 cheers` or the specific reaction breakdown.

## 08 · Panic hide, always available

A long-press on the home indicator hides SheRISE behind a **neutral Calculator disguise screen**. Safety is a UX pattern, not a settings menu item. The disguise must survive casual inspection: a working calculator with realistic history, no branding, no traces in the app switcher preview.
